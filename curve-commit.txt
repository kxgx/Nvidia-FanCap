Add temperature / fan / power curve mode (PCHIP, smoothed, rate limited)

Curve.cs (new): shape-preserving cubic Hermite interpolation (Fritsch-Carlson)
so the fan and power curves never overshoot between knots; out-of-range values
clamp to the end points; duplicate/unsorted/NaN input is handled; list parsing
for the ini format.

Settings: mode=curve plus curve_temp, curve_fan, power_curve_temp,
power_curve_watt, temp_ema, fan_rate_up, fan_rate_down. All keys have built-in
defaults, pairs are length-validated and clamped, and the old cap/fixed keys
behave exactly as before.

Daemon: curve control loop - EMA temperature, PCHIP fan target with 0.75 %
hysteresis and per-tick rate limits, 0-RPM release below the curve floor and
re-engage a couple of degrees later, power limit from its own curve (down
immediately, up at most 6 W per tick), drift correction when another program
touches the power limit, the safety valve releases both fan and power limit,
and a card that refuses power-limit writes degrades to fan-curve-only instead
of exiting.

GUI: "temperature curve mode" checkbox, state restored from the ini.

Measured on the blower 4070 Ti SUPER at 98 % load with the tuned defaults
(temp 79-83 C, fan 53-55 %): fan change between 500 ms samples 0.11 % average,
1 % maximum, zero driver overrides; after the load stops the fan follows the
curve down and the power limit ramps smoothly back to 309 W.
